# Willcord brand

Willcord is the product name, and also the name of the repository, packages, CLI, services and configuration paths.

The original brand assets in this repository are included under Apache-2.0. The software license does not grant rights to use product names or marks to imply endorsement; see section 6 of LICENSE. This statement does not assert a registered trademark.

Forks should identify themselves clearly and should not imply that they are an official Willcord release. When modifying the product, use a distinct name and visual identity to avoid confusion.

Naming and visual similarity checks remain incomplete; see `docs/brand/naming-shortlist.md`.
